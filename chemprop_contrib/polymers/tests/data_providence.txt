data/dataset-poly_chemprop_small.csv and data/test_polymer.csv are taken from the original polymer
PR to Chemprop (https://github.com/chemprop/chemprop/pull/1214, tests/data/regression/polymer/ and
tests/data/). Both are subsets of the vinyl copolymer electron affinity (EA) and ionization potential
(IP) dataset from M. Aldeghi and C. W. Coley, "A graph representation of molecular ensembles for
polymer property prediction", Chem. Sci., 2022, 13, 10486-10498, DOI 10.1039/D2SC02839E. The full
dataset is available at
https://github.com/coleygroup/polymer-chemprop-data/blob/main/datasets/vipea/chemprop_inputs/dataset-poly_chemprop.csv

data/config.toml is the training config from the same PR, with data-path pointing to the file above
(relative to the root of the chemprop-contrib repository, where the tests are run from).
